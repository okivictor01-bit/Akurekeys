AkureKeysPrivacy Policy

Your data, respected 🔐

What we collect, why, and who sees it — in plain language, per the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

1. What we collect

• Account data: email, name, phone, role.
• Landlord data: property details, addresses, bank account.
• Agent data: referral activity, bank account.
• Tenant data: bookings, payments, releases.
• Technical data: device and usage logs.

2. How we use it

To run the service: verify listings, schedule inspections, hold and release escrow, pay out earnings, prevent fraud, and provide support. We do not sell your data.

3. Payments

Card payments and bank transfers are processed by Paystack (a licensed Nigerian payment institution). Card numbers never touch our servers; Paystack's own privacy policy applies to payment data.

4. Storage

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL with row-level security) and served via Cloudflare. Access is restricted by role; admin functions are protected.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage only to keep you signed in and to remember a referral link. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Sharing

We share only what the service needs: e.g., an agent sees landlords they onboarded; admin sees listings for verification; Paystack sees payment details. We never share data for marketing by third parties.

7. Retention

We keep records as long as your account is active and as required by law (financial records). You may request deletion subject to legal retention duties.

8. Your rights (NDPR)

You may access, correct, or request deletion of your data, and object to processing, by contacting admin. We respond within a reasonable time.

9. Security

Encryption in transit, role-based access control, server-side payment verification, and webhook signature checks protect your data and money. No system is perfectly secure; report vulnerabilities to admin.
