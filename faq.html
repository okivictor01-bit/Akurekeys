Questions? Answered. 💬

Everything tenants, landlords and agents ask us.

What is escrow and why does it matter?

Your rent is held safely by the platform — not given to the landlord — until you inspect and confirm you're satisfied. Nobody can run with your money.

Why pay ₦1,000 to unlock a listing?

It filters scammers and time-wasters, and protects landlords' phone numbers. You get the full address, photos/details, and our admin contact instead.

Why is there an inspection fee?

It guarantees serious bookings. Our admin personally confirms your slot with the landlord before you go.

When does the landlord get paid?

Only after YOU release the escrow. Then 94% goes to the landlord automatically, 3.5% to the platform, 2.5% to the agent.

What if I'm not satisfied after inspection?

Don't release the money. Contact admin (0800-KEYS) and we mediate. Your rent stays protected in escrow.

How do I list my property?

Landlord dashboard → "Add a property" → admin verifies and approves → your listing goes live to thousands of tenants.

How do agents earn?

Share your referral link. When a landlord registers through it, you're attached forever and earn 2.5% of every rent on their properties.

Is my money safe?

Payments run on Paystack (a licensed Nigerian payment institution). Escrow logic runs on secure servers with bank-grade database protection.

How do I reach support?

📞 0800-KEYS — the only contact number on the platform. We never ask you to pay anyone directly.
